HOW TO PLAY

On the ground: W accelerate · A/D steer · S brake
SPACE hold to charge a jump, release to launch
Q tail grab · E nose grab · SHIFT indy grab (hold in the air)
In the air: W backflip · S frontflip · A spin left · D spin right
B+W nose butter · B+S tail butter · add A/D to spin
Hold R restart run · ESC pause

Land with your rotation close to a half or full turn. Jump onto rails and boxes to grind and balance with A/D. Trees, bad landing angles and lost balance cause crashes. Blue patches are icy.
